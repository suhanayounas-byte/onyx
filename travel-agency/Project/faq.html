Booking & payment

A 20% deposit confirms your seats, hotel rooms and any internal flights. The balance is due 30 days before departure, and we can split it into two or three instalments on request. All funds sit in a protected trust account until your trip is completed.

Bank transfer, Visa, Mastercard and American Express, with no card-processing fee added. For bookings over $6,000 we also accept payment split across two currencies if you hold accounts in USD and AED or GBP.

Every price on the site is per person on a twin-sharing basis and already includes airport taxes, city taxes and transfers. Solo travellers pay a single-room supplement, listed on each package page.

Yes. We hold most group departures for 48 hours at no cost, and international air fares for 24 hours where the airline permits it. Nothing is charged during a hold.

Flights, visas & documents

Package prices exclude international flights so you can use miles, fly from a different city or choose your own cabin. We hold IATA ticketing authority and will quote flights alongside your itinerary, usually within two hours.

We prepare and submit visa documentation for the UAE, Turkey, Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Schengen countries and Japan. You receive a checklist, an appointment booking where required, and a covering letter with your confirmed itinerary. Government fees are paid separately.

Call the 24/7 line printed on your itinerary. Our desk rebooks flights, extends hotel nights and reschedules transfers directly. Eight out of ten disruptions are resolved within an hour, and you are never charged for our time.

Your mobile itinerary is live as soon as you confirm and updates automatically. Final vouchers, tickets, guide contacts and offline maps are added 14 days before departure.

On the trip

Group departures are capped at 18 travellers, and several itineraries such as the Maldives honeymoon and Norway aurora trip run at 12 or fewer. Fully private departures are available on any package for a supplement.

Yes, insurance is a condition of travel on all our packages. You may use your own policy if it covers medical evacuation of at least $100,000, or buy through us from $19 per traveller with cover up to $250,000.

Halal, vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and nut-allergy requirements are recorded on your booking and passed to every hotel, restaurant and airline in advance. Tell us at booking so we can confirm each supplier in writing.

Balloon flights are grounded by the civil aviation authority in high wind. We build a spare morning into the Cappadocia itinerary so your flight is rebooked the next day. If it cannot fly at all, the flight cost is refunded in full.

Yes. Our Malaysia, Thailand, Dubai and Switzerland packages are family-designed, with connecting rooms, child seats in every vehicle, shorter touring days and reduced pricing for ages 2 to 11.

Changes & support

More than 45 days before departure: full refund less a $75 administration fee. 44–30 days: 80% refund. 29–15 days: 50% refund. Within 14 days the booking is non-refundable, although name changes are allowed up to 7 days before departure.

One date change is free of charge if requested more than 45 days before departure, subject to availability and any airline fare difference. Later changes fall under the cancellation policy.

Almost every booking we take is adjusted in some way: extra nights, an upgraded hotel category, a private guide instead of a group, or an added city. Send us the package you like and describe the changes; a revised quote follows within 24 hours.

The 24/7 traveller line is +1 (415) 220-8899, answered in under 90 seconds on average. Your assigned designer is also reachable on WhatsApp, and your tour manager travels with the group throughout.
