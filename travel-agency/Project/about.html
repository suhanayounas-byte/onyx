Our story

From one desk in San Francisco to 24 countries

TravelVista began in 2009 when Maya Ellington, a former airline revenue analyst, started planning Turkey and Greece itineraries for colleagues who were tired of piecing trips together across six booking sites. The first year produced 74 bookings from a single rented desk on Harbour Street.

Seventeen years later we are a team of 40 across San Francisco, Dubai and Istanbul, operating guided departures in 24 countries and serving more than 48,000 travellers. What has not changed is the working method: one designer owns your file, and nothing goes into an itinerary that a member of our team has not personally experienced.

  • IATA accredited agency, licence #TV-4471
  • Client funds held in a protected trust account until travel is completed
  • Average designer experience of 9 years and 31 countries visited
  • 96% of travellers rate their trip 5 out of 5 after returning

Our vision

To be the travel company people return to for a decade of holidays, not a single transaction — with 60% of our bookings already coming from repeat travellers and their referrals.

Responsible travel

We cap group sizes at 18, contract locally owned hotels wherever possible, and offset the carbon of every flight we issue through verified reforestation projects in Kenya and Indonesia.
