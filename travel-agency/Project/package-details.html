Turkey Cultural Explorer

8 days across Istanbul and Cappadocia — Ottoman monuments, a Bosphorus crossing between two continents and a sunrise balloon flight over the fairy chimneys.

Tour overview

Turkey Cultural Explorer is our most requested European departure, and the itinerary is built around two very different halves. The first four days are urban: Byzantine and Ottoman monuments, markets, ferries and street food in a city of 16 million people spread across two continents.

The second half moves inland to Cappadocia, where you stay in a genuine cave hotel carved into volcanic rock in Göreme. Here the pace slows — valley walks, an underground city, a pottery workshop in Avanos, and the sunrise balloon flight that most travellers describe as the highlight of their year.

Because the balloon flight depends on wind conditions, we build a spare morning into the schedule. If your slot is cancelled by the civil aviation authority, we rebook it the following day at no extra cost, or refund the flight in full.

Trip highlights

  • Sunrise hot-air balloon flight over the fairy chimneys with flight certificate
  • Two nights in an authentic cave hotel in Göreme
  • Guided visits to Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque and Topkapi Palace
  • Bosphorus cruise plus a ferry crossing to the Asian side of Istanbul
  • Kaymaklı underground city and a 3 km guided walk in Ihlara Valley
  • Home-cooked village lunch in Belisirma and a farewell Dervish performance

Who this trip suits

Suitable for travellers aged 8 and above with a moderate fitness level — expect 6–8 km of walking on city days and uneven ground in the valleys. Not recommended for travellers who cannot manage stairs, as the underground city has narrow, low passages.

Day-by-day itinerary

Hotel check-in is 2:00 PM and checkout 12:00 PM. All internal flights and transfers are included.

Day 1

Arrival in Istanbul & Bosphorus welcome dinner

Private transfer from Istanbul Airport to your hotel in Sultanahmet. After check-in and a rest, meet your tour manager at 7:00 PM for a welcome dinner on the Bosphorus waterfront with views of the Maiden's Tower.

Day 2

Old City: Hagia Sophia, Blue Mosque & Topkapi Palace

A full guided walking day through the Historic Peninsula — Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, the Hippodrome and Topkapi Palace with its Treasury and Harem quarters. Lunch of Ottoman mezze is included, and the afternoon ends in the Grand Bazaar.

Day 3

Bosphorus cruise, Spice Bazaar & Asian side

Morning cruise along the Bosphorus past Dolmabahce Palace and the Rumeli Fortress, then the Spice Bazaar for Turkish delight and saffron. Afternoon ferry to Kadıköy on the Asian side for a street-food tasting walk. Evening free.

Day 4

Flight to Cappadocia & Göreme Open Air Museum

Morning flight from Istanbul to Kayseri, then a 70-minute drive to Göreme. Check into your cave hotel before visiting the Göreme Open Air Museum, with its rock-cut churches and 11th-century frescoes. Sunset at Red Valley viewpoint.

Day 5

Sunrise balloon flight & Devrent Valley

A 4:45 AM pickup for your one-hour hot-air balloon flight over the fairy chimneys, finishing with the traditional certificate and sparkling toast. Later, Devrent and Pasabag valleys, plus a pottery workshop in Avanos on the Red River.

Day 6

Underground city, Ihlara Valley & local lunch

Descend eight levels into the Kaymaklı underground city, then walk a 3 km section of the Ihlara Valley gorge beside the Melendiz stream. A home-cooked lunch in Belisirma village is included before returning to Göreme.

Day 7

Return to Istanbul & free afternoon

Morning flight back to Istanbul with an afternoon at leisure — optional Turkish bath at a restored 16th-century hamam, or shopping in Nisantasi. Farewell dinner with a Whirling Dervish performance.

Day 8

Departure

Breakfast at the hotel, late checkout where available, and a private transfer to Istanbul Airport timed to your flight. Your tour manager remains reachable until you land home.
